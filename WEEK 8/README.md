# HairMela Security and Data Protection Audit

SIWES project by **Ayeni Ayomide** (Cybersecurity Trainee, Axiahub). A security and data protection audit of HairMela, a salon walk-in queue app, using **NIST CSF 2.0** and the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023**. Done with HairMela's written permission, using public information, the app as a normal customer, and a survey answered by HairMela's management. No testing was done.

**Result:** 98%, Strong, as reported by HairMela's management (not verified against documents). One High risk, eight Medium and five Low; the public pages and app are thinner than management reports in several places.

## Data handling

Raw survey responses are not published. Answers are kept confidential and summarised. Screenshots are redacted.

## Limits

Documents were not seen, so "Implemented" means management-reported. Legal section references should be confirmed in the official Act. Not legal advice.
